---
name: isbn-guidance
description: Explains ISBN requirements and options for a given platform and format (does the platform require the customer's own ISBN, offer a free platform-assigned one, or not require one at all for this format), and helps the customer decide. Use when the customer asks about ISBNs, when a submission package is missing one a platform requires, or when the customer is publishing to multiple platforms and needs to know whether one ISBN covers all of them. Do NOT use to actually register or purchase an ISBN on the customer's behalf — this is guidance only; ISBN purchase, if needed, happens on the customer's own account outside this system.
---

## Steps
1. Determine the target platform(s) and format(s) (print vs. ebook —
   requirements often differ for the same platform).
2. Explain, per platform: free platform-assigned ISBN available? Own
   ISBN required? Does one ISBN work across multiple platforms, or does
   each need its own?
3. If the customer needs to obtain their own ISBN, name that this
   happens outside this system, on their own account with their
   country's ISBN agency — do not imply the platform or this product
   purchases or registers one.
4. Record the customer's choice against the book's publishing record so
   `platform-metadata-mapper` can use it.

## Edge cases
- Customer is publishing the same book to multiple platforms with
  different ISBN policies (e.g. one requires their own, one offers
  free): lay out the tradeoff plainly (a shared own-ISBN gives one
  consistent identifier across platforms; free assigned ISBNs are
  platform-specific and not portable) rather than picking for them.
