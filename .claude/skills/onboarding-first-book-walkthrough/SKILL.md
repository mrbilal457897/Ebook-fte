Do NOT use for an existing customer with books already in progress asking a specific how-to question about a later stage (formatting, publishing) — route those to search_help_library or the relevant specialist instead.

## Steps
1. Acknowledge the hesitation directly and briefly, without dwelling on
   it — reassure, then move to action.
2. Ask one simple, low-pressure question to get started: a rough topic,
   genre, or even just "fiction or nonfiction" — not a long
   questionnaire.
3. Explain, in one or two sentences, what happens next (their content
   will be generated and they'll watch it happen, they can revise
   anything) so the process feels concrete, not opaque.
4. Hand off to the Orchestrator to route to the Content Agent's outline
   step once the customer has enough of a starting point.
5. Mention free-tier usage plainly and early (they get generations to
   try before any cost decision), so cost anxiety doesn't surface later
   as a surprise.

## Edge cases
- Customer expresses they don't have a book idea at all, just interest
  in "having written a book": offer a short list of common starting
  angles (a memoir prompt, a how-to based on their stated expertise, a
  genre-fiction premise generator) rather than leaving them stuck on an
  open-ended question.
